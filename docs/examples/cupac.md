# CUPAC

Train and freeze a predictor on separate historical units, score experiment units
before assignment, and use the stored score as a CUPED covariate. The notebook
compares decision and sensitivity results and shows what happens when one arm
loses scores selectively.

[Open the notebook in a full browser tab](cupac.html) or read the [CUPAC recipe](../guides/cupac.md).
