# Proposal: a separate two-hypothesis family (not an Increment API)

For metric $j$, the sharp null fixes each unit's binary outcome: $Y_{ij}(1)=Y_{ij}(0)$. Condition on all potential outcomes, assume no interference, and randomize independently with $A_i\sim\mathrm{Bernoulli}(1/2)$. Predeclare units, reveal order, both metrics, positive agreement direction, horizon, family level and bet. Reveal the assignment and both final outcomes together; no missing, revised, selected or arm-dependent reveals. Dependence between metrics on a unit is allowed. The only randomness in the enumeration is assignment, not sampling outcomes or permuting within fixed arm totals.

- **One look.** Under each true sharp null, $I_{ij}=1\{A_i=Y_{ij}\}$ are independent fair bits across units. At a prespecified $n$, $k_j=\sum I_{ij}$ and $p_j=2^{-n}\sum_{r=k_j}^n {n\choose r}$; discreteness makes this upper-tail p super-uniform, not necessarily uniform. Bonferroni rejects $p_j\le\alpha/2$. The union bound requires no independence between hypotheses. Trying every $n$ reuses the same allocation and loses the fixed-look guarantee. [Branson & Bind, *Randomization-Based Inference for Bernoulli-Trial Experiments*, §§2–3](https://arxiv.org/abs/1707.04136); [Holm (1979), classical Bonferroni comparison](https://www.jstor.org/stable/4615733).
- **Monitoring.** Before each joint reveal fix the stake at $1/2$. Update $E_{j,0}=1$, $E_{j,i}=E_{j,i-1}[1+\tfrac12(2A_i-1)(2Y_{ij}-1)]$. Even given the joint past, a true null gives multipliers $3/2$ and $1/2$ with equal probability. Thus $E$ is a nonnegative martingale. Ville gives $P(\exists i:E_{j,i}\ge 2/\alpha)\le\alpha/2$; summing over **true** nulls bounds the chance of any false rejection across metrics and looks. Record whether each metric ever crossed. The running maximum is **not** a current e-value. [Shafer, Shen, Vereshchagin & Vovk (2011), §1, Ville's inequality](https://doi.org/10.1214/10-STS347).

**Integration: not admissible as-is.** [`SequentialRegistration`, `JointReveal`, `SequentialCell`, `sequential_family_rule`](../../../increment/semantics/sequential.py) bind definitions, source, reveal law, typed models and roster before data; the unsegmented exact family selects by e-BH (FDR), not this first-crossing Bonferroni rule (FWER). [`EValueEvidence.__post_init__`](../../../increment/estimation/decision_types.py) requires a matching Bernoulli checkpoint, recomputed likelihood certificate, hypothesis and `raw_likelihood_v1` process. Binary data alone do not make this sign bet that likelihood. [`family_evidence`/`combine_family_evidence`](../../../increment/result_envelope.py) distinguish typed p from e evidence, exclude non-decision methods and use **current** log-e for e-BH. [`decide`](../../../increment/decide/evaluator.py) matches stored registration, bound plan and snapshot-scoped members. A free-standing p/e column cannot join the family.

Conceptually feasible only with a newly registered sharp-null procedure identity, explicit family/error budget, compatible joint filtration, finalized append-only capture (cf. [`SequentialSnapshot`](../../../increment/sequential_state.py)), construction-specific checkpoint validation and a decision adapter. A separate family does not extend a built-in family's error budget: taking either family's rejection as a launch rule would require joint allocation. No conclusion here covers a weak mean null, arbitrary estimator, rolling revision, or nominal Mann–Whitney result.
