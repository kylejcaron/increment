# Fraction harmed decision study, protocol v1

Frozen before observing campaign or field outcomes. This study asks whether a randomized binary-outcome experiment can rule in or rule out an unacceptable fraction of the population losing a beneficial outcome. Its output is a research recommendation, not an estimator in `increment`, an individual risk score, a public interface, or a launch decision. Record protocol and source hashes before evaluating outcomes. The source baseline is `77ab667b227b2005e18bcc3d0f56c23b6aaec651`.

## Target and identification

**Population.** Independent units sampled from one named superpopulation at common entry, constant positive two-arm randomization independent of covariates and both potential outcomes (Bernoulli assignment or complete randomization independent of the unit records), no spillover, one version per arm, consistency, no attrition, and one complete binary outcome at one fixed window, with 1 explicitly beneficial on the named measure. For complete randomization, condition on the realized assignment vector; the draws of units are still random. No claim conditional on fixed individual potential outcomes, no clustered or covariate-dependent allocations, censoring, targeting by exposure, external transport, survey weighting, or sequential looks. Assignment probability is design evidence, not fit from observed arm sizes. Unknown or incompatible design gives no interval. `X=(S,B)` consists of two prespecified binary baseline flags. Extra covariates in a file do not make this the sharp set given all its information.

**Identification.** Let `h=P(Y(0)=1,Y(1)=0)`. In leaf `g`, `p=P(Y(0)=1|g)` and `q=P(Y(1)=0|g)`; `q` is a failure rate. The joint probability of these events is at most either marginal, and their union has probability at most one. Thus `l=max(0,p+q-1)`, `u=min(p,q)`. The four masses in `(00,01,10,11)` order for any `v∈[l,u]` are `(q-v,1-p-q+v,v,p-v)`: all nonnegative with the prescribed marginals. With common target weights `w_g=P(g)`, `L=Σw_g l_g`, `U=Σw_g u_g` (couple independently within each leaf and mix). Refinement cannot lower population L or raise population U by convexity of max and concavity of min. A positive mean effect `P(Y(1)=1)-P(Y(0)=1)` can coexist with positive h. No monotonicity, independence of the potential outcomes, CATE-to-harm conversion, or harm labels. Distinguish the population identification span, sample plug-in endpoint estimates (which can be biased), and sampling intervals for the two endpoints. Only the outer sampling endpoints make decisions. More samples can learn `[L,U]`, not select an element of it.

## Five methods, one family

- `U0`: unstratified; `F`: fixed S; `Q`: fixed full `(S,B)`. Each uses all units.
- `H`: honest nuisance-derived partition; `M`: its deliberately misspecified control. Randomly order units independently of data. Use first `floor(n/3)` for learning and the rest for estimation, sharing the split between H and M. For H fit each arm's binary **success** rate for each of the four training atoms with `(y+1)/(n_a+2)` (Laplace prediction even with no training observations). Use tag `(S, m0>m1, m0+m1>1)` with integer cross-products, strict comparisons, and ties false. Equal tags pool, including tags of atoms not observed in evaluation. M fits only arm-by-S, broadcast to both B values in S, then follows the identical downstream procedure. Label leaves by first appearance scanning `00,01,10,11`. Store the full map and both split sizes. No prior counts enter evaluation.

Given training, H and M are fixed partitions on independent evaluation units. Any fitted means, even wrong ones, only decide what to pool. The result is sharp for that coarsening and an outer set for the full-feature set, not necessarily full-feature sharp or efficient. F vs M measures lost sample; U0/F/Q vs H includes the cost of fitting. This is not Kallus Algorithm 1. Do not select cuts, features, or split seed on evaluation data, average fold-wise endpoints with a pooled Wald standard error, use the ordinary bootstrap at ties, or take the narrower of several uncorrected intervals.

## Sampling bands and decisions

For each method's map with `K` distinct possible leaves, allocate `δ=.05/(5·6K)` to each of two tails of each of `3K` binomial coordinates: control successes and treatment failures (conditional on observed arm/leaf counts), and leaf membership among all units in the method's estimation sample. A coordinate with `k` of `n>0` has Clopper–Pearson (CP) lower `0` at `k=0`, else `beta.ppf(δ,k,n-k+1)`; upper `1` at `k=n`, else `beta.isf(δ,k+1,n-k)`. Never use `1-alpha` quantiles; sum small probabilities with log tails or exact rationals. The interval of a randomly empty arm-leaf is `[0,1]`; count it, and if the leaf could have positive mass set both plug-in endpoints to null with `empty_arm_leaf`. An empty study, invalid counts, or design nonpositivity is not a randomly empty cell. For `K=1` the weight is exactly 1; do not reallocate unused tails.

- **Known mass:** Use exact masses of the trial's same superpopulation as stipulated by the design or generating law, summed over training-defined tags. They are not empirical enrollment fractions, arm-specific weights, or external cohort weights with unmatched within-stratum mix. There is no finite-roster guarantee from calling empirical weights known.
- **Estimated mass:** Pool arms for `ŵ_g=n_g/N`. Project over `W={Σw=1, w^-_g≤w_g≤w^+_g}`, where the limits are simultaneous prevalence CP intervals. Include leaves with zero observed membership. The box contains the empirical simplex point. Infeasibility beyond numerical tolerance is an execution failure, never repaired by dropping strata.
- With documented true same-population weights, both modes on a draw share the same coordinate event, with no double spending. Without that documentation use estimated mass only.

Both hinges are increasing in p and q. Compute `l^-`, `l^+`, `u^-`, `u^+` at componentwise lower and upper limits. Report `[min_W w·l^-, max_W w·l^+]` for L and `[min_W w·u^-, max_W w·u^+]` for U. With fixed weights use dot products. Otherwise start at lower limits and allocate mass up to the caps in ascending coefficient order to minimize, descending to maximize, with canonical index for ties. The separate extrema cover separate functionals over the *same feasible set*, not endpoints from different target populations. Retain both ranges, plug-in pair, outer `[L_lo,U_hi]`, sample width, and counts. When `L_hi<U_lo`, the intervening span is known ambiguous for that particular partition on the simultaneous event; it is not a band for h.

Given training where used, the arm outcomes in a leaf are binomial conditional on counts, and the evaluation leaf count is marginally binomial. Constant randomization independent of unit records permits pooling within a leaf. Union bound per method, integrate over training, and union bound across methods; independence between methods is unnecessary. In real arithmetic all L/U ranges cover their own population endpoints jointly with probability at least .95, including ties and boundaries. The outer ranges then cover h. This is not exact equality to .95, a posterior, or an unconditional guarantee for arbitrary logs. Floating-point inversion is audited separately, not claimed to provide a machine-precision enclosure.

For a band `[a,b]` and `0<t<1`, **clear** only if `b<t`, **rule_out** only if `a>t`, otherwise **inconclusive**, including equality. Do not round before deciding; treat comparisons within `1e-12` absolute of equality as inconclusive. The nested decision pipelines use intersections of outer bands only: `P0=U0`, `P1=U0∩F`, `P2=P1∩Q`, `P3=P2∩H`. M never influences a decision. On the same event all pipelines contain the full `(S,B)` identified set, so threshold comparisons do not incur repeated testing. Disjointness means abstention with `discordant_bands` and counts as simultaneous noncoverage; never reverse or sort the endpoints. Nothing here gives decisions on future looks, metrics, alternative campaigns, or subgroups.

## Exact numerical checks

Enumerate all nonnegative integer potential-outcome tables of size eight and compare each marginal pair's complete harm lattice with the interval. Require 165 tables and 81 marginal pairs, including `(4,4)` with harm counts `{0,1,2,3,4}`. Cross all 1,225 ordered pairs of size-four tables as equally weighted strata and compare extrema conditional on their separate margins. Success margins `(1,3),(3,1)` give `{2,3,4}` out of eight versus `{0,1,2,3,4}` pooled. Include all harm, pure benefit, and irrelevant covariates. Compare the greedy simplex projections to independently enumerated vertices for one through four leaves, including degeneracy, tied coefficients, and a zero-frequency leaf.

For `(n0,n1)=(4,4),(9,11),(25,20)` and `p,q∈{0,.01,.2,.4,.5,.99,1}`, sum exact binomial count-pair masses and compare each CP endpoint to 80-step bisection on finite rational binomial tail sums, with exact zero/one boundaries. Record simultaneous L/U endpoint coverage and false threshold actions relative to independent potential-table truth, not merely coverage of h. For `(25,20,.2,.4)` require coordinate acceptance ranges `0..11` and `2..14` and joint coordinate mass `.996327663382` (absolute `1e-11`). Projected coverage must be at least the corresponding box probability. There is no Monte Carlo tolerance. Require zero mismatches and record all coverage minima and worst coordinates.

## Synthetic campaign

Atoms are ordered `(S,B)=00,01,10,11`. Each tuple is the joint potential-outcome **percentages** in `(00,01,10,11)` order, not independent draws of potential outcomes. Let `T=(43,47,0,10)`, `V=(5,0,25,70)`. Atom weights are equal except D6, whose weights are `(.49,.49,.01,.01)`.

| DGP | Atom laws in order | True h | Population U0; F; Q sets |
|---|---|---:|---|
| D0: observational twin, no harm | all `(50,0,0,50)` | 0 | all `[0,.5]` |
| D1: same observed law, substantial harm | all `(0,50,50,0)` | .5 | all `[0,.5]` |
| D2: all harmed | all `(0,0,100,0)` | 1 | all `[1,1]` |
| D3: all benefited | all `(0,100,0,0)` | 0 | all `[0,0]` |
| D4: informative S, favorable average | `T,T,V,V` | .125 | `[0,.365]; [.125,.20]; [.125,.20]` |
| D5: informative B ignored by M, favorable average | `T,V,T,V` | .125 | `[0,.365]; [0,.365]; [.125,.20]` |
| D6: rare S, exact/near ties, sparse/extreme rates | `(98,1,1,0),(0,1,1,98),(1,48,49,2),(2,49,48,1)` | .0195 | `[0,.5]; [0,.5]; [.0001,.0197]` |

Use both same-target mass modes for every method. Derive truth for H/M conditional on the realized map, not from Q endpoints. For any map form exact rational leaf marginal masses from the DGP, clear denominators per leaf, enumerate possible `11` counts, derive remaining cells from the margins, retain nonnegative tables, and minimize/maximize `10`. Standardize with true leaf prevalences; cache by `(DGP,map)`. True h comes from the `10` masses. Compare true-policy, full-feature identification, method identification, sample plug-in, and outer conclusions separately.

Cross all seven DGPs with `n∈(160,4096)` and `e=P(A=1)∈(.5,.1)`, in that order: 28 cells with **20,000 independent trials per cell**. For zero-based `(d,n_index,e_index,trial,split)`, use `Generator(PCG64(SeedSequence([20261119082,d,n_index,e_index,trial,split])))` with `split=0,1` for independent train/evaluation. For each split draw one multinomial over lexicographic `(S,B,Y0,Y1,A)` with probabilities `w_sb * joint_sb[y0,y1] * P(A)` and sizes `floor(n/3), n-floor(n/3)`. Use only the factual `Y=Y_A` in empirical estimation; pass no DGP weights in estimated mode. Share each trial across methods. Batch at most 128 trials, group repeated CP inversions at equal `(k,n,K)`, and aggregate ordered counts online. The smoke run takes trials `0..7` in every cell, labeled `certification_eligible=false`; it does not supply pass evidence. Selecting a subset never changes streams; never reroll, suppress a failed seed, or replace a failed estimate with h.

Per method/mode report attempts, valid bands, null point estimates and reasons, empty cells, bias and RMSE with denominators, each endpoint and joint coverage against oracle truth, h coverage, true identification span, mean estimated identification span, outer and inner spans, sampling excess `width(outer)-width(true ID)`, and paired changes vs U0/F. For each pipeline and method report abstention and wrong decisions per *attempted* trial at `(.0025,.005,.01,.025,.05,.10,.125,.15,.20,.25,.30,.32,.50)`, including exact-equality cases. False clearance is `clear && h>=t`; false rule-out is `rule_out && h<=t`. Also record false claims against identification (`clear && U>=t`, `rule_out && L<=t`; for intersections use full Q truth). Split abstentions into true identification straddling, sampling despite separated identification, and diagnostic. Null plug-in is not missing band; a missing required band, NaN, or exception makes the campaign incomplete/failed, not covered or abstaining. For hypothetical simulated loss use `C_wrong_clear:C_wrong_rule_out:C_abstain` of `10:1:.25` and `1:10:.25`, zero for correct decisions. Report paired differences vs never decide and vs P0 with Monte Carlo standard errors, not as inferred stakeholder costs.

**88 prespecified counters.** In each of the 28 cells count trials with (i) any L or U sampling interval missing its own true endpoint across all five methods and both modes, or any disjoint pipeline; (ii) any false clearance over all reported modes, methods, pipelines and thresholds; (iii) any false rule-out likewise. Each is a union event per trial. Four other counters measure success, all in estimated-mass mode with `n=4096,e=.5`:

| DGP | Positive-control success |
|---|---|
| D2 | P0 rules out at `.50` |
| D3 | P0 clears at `.50` |
| D4 | P0 is inconclusive and P1 clears at `.32` |
| D5 | P1 is inconclusive and `P1∩H` clears at `.32` |

Report the H-only increment `P1∩H` including its errors alongside the others. The last two controls require improving a decision, not just shrinking a band. M need not tighten; report why H loses when the full-data Q is better.

Report exact two-sided CP limits for each counter with `γ=.01/(2·88)` per tail, giving Monte Carlo family confidence at least .99 even with dependence. Error gate green iff upper ≤.05 (`≤882/20000` errors), red iff lower >.05 (`≥1122`), otherwise inconclusive. At actual error .06 the red trigger probability is `.99079888`. Power gate green iff lower ≥.80 (`≥16218/20000` successes), red iff upper <.80 (`≤15779`), otherwise unresolved. The .80 benchmarks constructed high-signal cases; it is not a stakeholder tolerance. Preserve excess abstention. A pointwise rate cannot certify all DGPs. Require no oracle mismatches, invalid numerics, missing coordinates, or excess false actions relative to their containing bound-miss event. A red coverage result triggers diagnosis on frozen inputs; red power questions utility, not the coverage theorem. Record completed cells and failures on all exits: full study `0` all green, `1` red/execution failure, `2` inconclusive.

## Independent field decision

The prespecified independent application is [Kevin Hillstrom's MineThatData field test](https://blog.minethatdata.com/2008/03/minethatdata-e-mail-analytics-and-data.html), distinct from the Kallus French trial. Compare assignment to `Mens E-Mail` versus `No E-Mail` among customers who bought in the prior 12 months; omit only the third randomized arm `Womens E-Mail` by design. Under the reported thirds the selected arms have equal conditional allocation. Use beneficial binary `conversion` (purchase within two weeks), `S=newbie`, `B=int(recency<=6)` in months before sending. Never condition on visit, email open, conversion, spend, or subsequent eligibility. Harm here is a lost purchase under the email, not a welfare judgment about receiving email. Use estimated weights, a common target across arms, and all retained rows. After selecting arms in immutable source-row order, apply one `Generator(PCG64(20261119083)).permutation(N)` and take first `N//3` for training. Source metadata give `N=42,613` and split `14,204/28,409`. Counts corroborate labels, not intact randomization. The historical release describes one row per customer but provides no stable customer ID; ordinals only locate records. Do not deduplicate on baseline features or claim the ordinal proves uniqueness. Require documentary unit/ITT, complete follow-up and attribution, comparability/no interference, and a defensible superpopulation interpretation. Disclose unverifiable assumptions. If only a historical benchmark is supported, label it and withhold current-population or operational conclusions.

**Access.** The source invites analysis but does not explicitly license general redistribution. The [TFDS checksum](https://github.com/tensorflow/datasets/blob/6ecc651e9519cb0c2a468f5eac4dea36819817f0/tensorflow_datasets/recommendation/hillstrom/checksums.tsv) corroborates bytes, not use rights; a code license is not a data grant. Official download is HTTP. Accept only locally staged, independently digest-checked data with authorized analysis and aggregate reporting. No CI fetch, raw redistribution, or disabling TLS. If rights, digest, schema, completeness, binary or assignment semantics fail, record the exact reason (e.g. `use_permission_unconfirmed`, `digest_mismatch`, `invalid_binary_outcome`, `unverified_trial_design`) and no case band. Do not substitute the checkout demo, French example or Criteo pooled privacy-subsampled uplift benchmark.

**Owner.** Before results, obtain dated authorization outside the repository and freeze a `DecisionCard` with `owner`, `decision_id`, `signed_at`, `unblind_at`, `target`, `arms`, `outcome`, `horizon`, `positive_direction`, `tau`, `max_false_action_risk`, `cost_false_clear`, `cost_false_rule_out`, `cost_abstain`, `incremental_workflow_cost`, `minimum_gain`, `currency_or_loss_unit`, `action_if_clear`, `action_if_rule_out`, `action_if_inconclusive`, `current_action`, `representativeness_evidence`, `permission_ref`. Require nonempty authority/evidence, signature strictly before unblinding, same target/direction/window, finite `0<tau<1`, nonnegative costs/minimum gain, strictly positive costs of both mistakes, and a risk ceiling accepting the simultaneous .05 guarantee. Pin the card SHA before opening outcomes. Obtain post-readout acknowledgment that the conditional action is operationally feasible and would be taken. Do not retroactively invent a historical owner or apply a decision to another retailer. Without an applicable prospective card, a permitted historical sweep is research only: `owner_decision=null`, `missing_decision_card`, no implement recommendation. For a different sponsor require their independent eligible trial and pre-outcome card; do not transport Hillstrom rates or hunt substitute datasets. For `owner_trial`, read a local CSV `unit_id,arm,y,s,b` with unique IDs, arm 0/1 and y/s/b 0/1, and manifest documenting rights and design. Stable sort by unique ID before permutation; apply the same estimand, split, engines, and checks. Duplicate IDs fail with `duplicate_unit`, not by keeping survivors.

Report each method/pipeline's plug-in and sampling results and threshold sensitivity, including owner `tau` if supplied. Name the smallest useful simple pipeline, prerequisites/costs, and whether H changes a real decision beyond full-data U0/F/Q. Counts, successes and mean differences are context only; a favorable average does not give clearance. One readout does not observe h or a false-clearance rate. Instead, for realized enclosing band B, proposed action d and frozen current action b, report `G_min=min_{z∈B}{loss(b,z)-loss(d,z)}-incremental_workflow_cost`. Map action choices to `clear/rule_out/abstain`; abstention always costs `cost_abstain`; a wrong strict claim costs its appropriate error cost including equality; correct claims cost zero. Enumerate only nonempty subregions `z<tau`, `z=tau`, `z>tau`. This is a conservative comparison on the coverage event, not posterior expected return: no guessed couplings, prior, or post-hoc tolerance. If B straddles tau, show the downside of guessing vs waiting. Distinguish sampling ambiguity from `L_hi<tau<U_lo` for Q, evidence of irreducibility at the declared feature resolution. State the actual alternatives and who bears costs. The false-action bound is about harm clearance, not general launch safety.

Input manifest fields: `csv_path,expected_sha256,permission_ref,design_ref,followup_ref,unit_ref,sampling_target,analysis_permitted,aggregates_permitted`. Before any outcome read enforce permission and digest. Missing manifest exits `2` with `missing_case_manifest` and `bounds=null`. Permissible data without an eligible card produce a `research_only` sweep and exit `2`. A completed field packet exits `0`; data-processing/execution error exits `1`. Do not open outcomes simply to fill an unavailable result. Retain the input manifest, bounds, thresholds and decision packet even if blocked, with numeric nulls and exact reasons, never the source rows.

## Recommendation

Verify the same protocol digest and file receipts across the exact-check, full campaign, and field packets. Require the post-readout acknowledgment when applicable. Missing evidence is not a pass; a positive, costed field result cannot override a failed safety gate, and a green campaign without a valid card must defer. Record both campaign and field packet digests, the simplest supported method, exact reasons, limits, and evidence that could change the conclusion. Do not access raw data or rerun simulations to join the packets.

- **Implement:** Recommend separately scoping only the simplest supported method changing a real, matched, prospectively specified action. Require exact checks and campaign safety/power gates green, permission/design gates, a conclusive actionable result at the owner's tolerance, post-readout acknowledgment, and `G_min>max(0,minimum_gain)`. Do not recommend learned adjustment without incremental decision value over fixed choices. Include gaps against the repository's eight method acceptance requirements: documented need and economic relevance, estimand/assumptions/guarantee, only raw fixed-horizon binary compatibility (no CUPED residual/ratio/winsor, clustering, sequential looks, subgroup declarations or general multiplicity), no planner/ingress parity, independent oracle, proposed refusals and missing public docs/contracts. Design approval and public engineering are separate decisions.
- **Defer:** Specify the missing owner/card/permission/representative trial, calibration precision, sample size or utility evidence, and its remedy. A valid inconclusive study is acceptable.
- **Reject:** Reject the construction only for a reproducible scientific counterexample to its guarantee within the stated domain, not a correctable bug. Reject for the named use case, not universally, if a validated full-feature inner interval strictly straddles the owner's tolerance and there are no other admissible baseline features, or if an adequately evidenced real comparison cannot meet the owner's action/cost criterion even in its best compatible state. Otherwise insufficient evidence means defer.

Preserve failed scenarios and null results. Exit `0` for evidenced implement/reject, `2` for defer with reasons, `1` for mismatched protocol digests or broken/corrupt inputs. A valid defer or reject is a study result.
